Real progress needs real science with real stakes.

What if you could have an RL environment that advances real scientific challenges with every run?

The problem

Benchmarks get gamed

Models overfit to known answers. Only open questions are a real test.

AI overrates itself

Agents that succeed only 22% of the time predict a 77% chance of success.1 They also misjudge their own costs.2

Null results vanish

65% of null results are never written up.3 No one learns from what failed.

1 Kaddour et al., ICLR 2026 · 2 Fradkin & Krishnan, MarketBench, 2026 · 3 Franco et al., Science 2014

A real-life example

Does DM-17 stop the growth of E. coli?

1 · Hypothesis

“Compound DM-17 inhibits growth of E. coli K-12 at 10 µg/mL.”

A significant effect or a solid null result wins the £500 prize.

2 · Design

The AI scientist plans four doses and two controls, three wells each.

3 · Bid

The run costs £150 against a £500 prize, so 60% is worth a bid.

4 · Result

Growth stays flat from 10 µg/mL: a conclusive yes.
