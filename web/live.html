Live market

A real AI model bets on a real question.

Pick a claim about a hidden world. A frontier model reads the prize and can pay to read earlier failed runs before bidding. Each round it pays a fee, may buy an experiment, and says how likely it is to get the answer right.

It ends with a verdict (supported, refuted or inconclusive), or it walks away. Only a correct, clear verdict wins the prize. The answer is hidden from the model and revealed here only at the end.
