Real lab data

Can an AI predict a measurement it hasn't seen?

Somebody in a lab measured how long each of 28,165 peptides stays attached to an immune receptor. We hid six of those measurements. An AI has to predict them, and it pays for every piece of evidence it looks at. Hiding the answer is the normal way to test a prediction. The change here is that being wrong costs it money.

The six questions

Each one asks whether a peptide stays attached for more than an hour. Three are yes and three are no, so guessing the same answer every time gets you half of them. On the two marked hard, most peptides on that receptor go the other way, so anything copying the usual pattern gets it wrong.
